# Stakeholder Questionnaire — Crimean Tatar Heritage Canada

> For the chairperson / leadership team. Plain-language, non-technical. Answer what you can; "not sure yet" is a perfectly good answer. Your responses turn our working assumptions into confirmed facts and set the scope for the first version of the website.
>
> **Essential** questions shape the first launch and are marked ⭐. **Optional / future** questions help longer-term planning and can wait.

---

## A. Organization identity ⭐

1. ⭐ What is the exact name you want shown to the public on the website?
2. ⭐ Is "Canada Crimea Cultural Committee" a former name, a related group, or unrelated? Is it still used anywhere (documents, social media, cheques)?
3. Are there other names, spellings, or abbreviations people know you by?
4. In one or two sentences, how would you describe the organization to someone who has never heard of it?

## B. Legal and public name ⭐

5. ⭐ What is your registered legal name (if incorporated)?
6. ⭐ Are you a registered charity or nonprofit? If so, what is your registration/business number? (Needed for donations and tax receipts.)
7. Who is legally authorized to approve website content and sign off on decisions?

## C. Mission and objectives ⭐

8. ⭐ What are the top three things you want this website to achieve in its first year?
9. ⭐ What would make you say, a year from now, "the website was a success"?
10. Is there anything you specifically do **not** want the website to be or do?

## D. Relationship with CACT ⭐

11. ⭐ How would you like the Canadian Association of Crimean Tatars (CACT) to be treated on the site — not mentioned, briefly acknowledged, or linked as a separate partner?
12. ⭐ Are there people, boards, or events shared between the two organizations that we should be careful about?
13. If we link to CACT, how would you like the relationship described in one sentence?

## E. Target audiences ⭐

14. ⭐ Of these, who matters **most** for launch (rank your top two): English-speaking Canadians new to the topic · younger diaspora members · older community members · teachers/students · researchers/journalists · cultural partners · donors/volunteers?
15. Who are you **not** trying to reach right now?
16. Are most of your audience in a particular city or province, or across Canada?

## F. Current programs ⭐

17. ⭐ What does the organization actually do today, in practice? (Events, screenings, translations, classes, gatherings, etc.)
18. Which of these programs is the most active or most important to feature first?
19. Are any programs paused, seasonal, or aspirational rather than current?

## G. Recurring events ⭐

20. ⭐ What events do you hold, and how often (annual festival, monthly meetups, screenings, commemorations)?
21. ⭐ Do you have a list of upcoming events with dates, locations, and descriptions we can publish?
22. Where do events usually take place (cities, venues)?
23. Do events need online registration or ticketing? Free or paid?

## H. Books and translations

24. What Crimean Tatar books have you translated into English, or plan to?
25. For each: title, author, translator, year, and whether it's available to buy, borrow, or read online.
26. Do you have permission to show book covers and describe/excerpt the works?

## I. Films and screenings

27. Which films have you subtitled or screened for English-speaking audiences?
28. For each: title, director, year, language, and whether you hold rights to subtitle/screen/show a trailer.
29. Can people request a screening or partnership? If so, how does that work today?

## J. Language initiatives

30. What language-preservation work do you do or plan (classes, phrases, recordings, dictionaries)?
31. Do you have any audio recordings of Crimean Tatar words, phrases, songs, or speakers?
32. Which writing system(s) do you use for Crimean Tatar (Latin, Cyrillic)?

## K. Educational work

33. Do you create materials for teachers or students? What kind?
34. Have you presented at schools, universities, libraries, or festivals?
35. Do you have partners in the education sector?

## L. Photography and permissions ⭐

36. ⭐ Do the two Facebook pages belong to the organization, and can we use the photos on the website?
37. ⭐ Who took the photos, and do you have permission from the people shown in them?
38. Do you have original, higher-quality copies of these photos (not just what's on Facebook)?
39. Are there any photos we must **not** publish, or people who should not be named?

## M. Oral histories

40. Have you recorded any community members' stories or memories (audio or video)?
41. Do you have consent from those individuals to share their stories publicly?
42. Are any stories sensitive and needing special care?

## N. Archive materials

43. Do you hold historical photographs, documents, artwork, or objects?
44. Where did they come from, and do you know who owns the rights?
45. Is anything already digitized?

## O. Branding ⭐

46. ⭐ Do you have a logo? Can you share the original files?
47. ⭐ Do you have specific colors, fonts, or a style you already use?
48. Is there existing artwork, letterhead, or event posters we should match?

## P. Logo and tamga ⭐

49. ⭐ Do you use the Crimean Tatar tamga symbol? In what form, and are there rules about how it must (and must not) be shown?
50. ⭐ Who provides and approves the official tamga/logo artwork?

## Q. Languages ⭐

51. ⭐ English will be first. Which language should come next — Crimean Tatar, French, or Ukrainian — and roughly when?
52. Do you have people who can translate and review website text into those languages?

## R. Website management ⭐

53. ⭐ After launch, who will keep the website updated (add events, photos, news)?
54. ⭐ How comfortable is that person with technology? How much time can they give?
55. How often do you expect to add or change content?

## S. Donations

56. Do you currently accept donations? Through what (bank, PayPal, CanadaHelps, in person)?
57. Do donors need tax receipts?
58. Is fundraising a priority for the first launch, or can it come later?

## T. Memberships

59. Do you have a formal membership program? Paid or free?
60. Should people be able to join or renew through the website?

## U. Volunteers

61. What do volunteers help with?
62. Should the website let people sign up to volunteer? Who receives those requests?

## V. Partners

63. Which organizations, institutions, or sponsors do you work with?
64. May we show their logos and link to them?

## W. Event registration

65. For paid or limited events, do you use any ticketing tool today (Eventbrite, etc.)?
66. Would you prefer registration on the website or links to an outside tool?

## X. Contact information ⭐

67. ⭐ What contact details should be public (email, phone, mailing address, social links)?
68. Who receives messages sent through a website contact form?

## Y. Launch priorities ⭐

69. ⭐ If the first version could include only a handful of sections, which matter most?
70. ⭐ Is there a target date or event you'd like the launch to align with?
71. What is the budget range for building and hosting the site, if known?

## Z. Long-term vision

72. Where would you like the website to be in three to five years?
73. Are there "dream features" (interactive map, timeline, full digital archive, phrase-of-the-day) you're excited about?
74. Is there anything important we haven't asked about?

---

### How to return this

Answer directly in this document, in an email, or in a call — whatever is easiest. Partial answers are welcome; we will follow up on anything unclear. Please **do not** send us other people's photos or copyrighted material until we've confirmed together that permissions are in place.
