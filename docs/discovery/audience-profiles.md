# Audience Profiles — Crimean Tatar Heritage Canada

> Status: **draft — preliminary profiles.** Audience **groups** are confirmed by the organization; **priority ranking is unresolved** (a stakeholder question). These are concise working profiles, not invented biographies. Confirm and refine after stakeholder input.

Each profile covers: motivation · likely entry point · key questions · desired content · usability needs · trust concerns · likely next action.

---

## 1. English-speaking Canadian, new to the topic

- **Motivation:** curiosity — heard the term, saw an event, or arrived via search/social; wants to understand who Crimean Tatars are.
- **Likely entry point:** Home, an "About the Crimean Tatars" explainer, or an event/gallery page shared on social media.
- **Key questions:** Who are the Crimean Tatars? Where are they from? What is this organization? Why does this matter in Canada?
- **Desired content:** a clear, welcoming overview; a few striking photographs; short history; upcoming events; films they could attend.
- **Usability needs:** plain English, minimal jargon, no assumed prior knowledge; easy scanning; strong mobile experience.
- **Trust concerns:** wants to know this is a credible cultural organization, not a political campaign; expects accurate, non-sensational information.
- **Likely next action:** read a bit more, view a gallery, or note an upcoming event/screening.

## 2. Younger Crimean Tatar diaspora member

- **Motivation:** connection to heritage and identity; may have partial knowledge and want to learn more or get involved.
- **Likely entry point:** social media link, events, language or music sections.
- **Key questions:** What's happening in the community? How do I reconnect with the language/culture? How can I participate or volunteer?
- **Desired content:** events, photos they recognize, language phrases, music, stories, volunteer opportunities; shareable, modern presentation.
- **Usability needs:** fast, mobile-first, visually engaging; easy sharing; not "dusty" or overly institutional.
- **Trust concerns:** authenticity — wants it to feel genuinely Crimean Tatar, not a generic template; represented respectfully, not as a stereotype.
- **Likely next action:** share content, attend/volunteer, explore language or stories.

## 3. Older community member

- **Motivation:** pride in the culture; staying connected; seeing familiar faces, events, and memories preserved.
- **Likely entry point:** direct link shared by family/community, or Events/Gallery.
- **Key questions:** When is the next gathering? Are our events and memories recorded? Is our history told correctly?
- **Desired content:** event details, photo galleries of community life, history and traditions, possibly in Crimean Tatar later.
- **Usability needs:** larger text, high contrast, big tap targets, simple navigation, no reliance on subtle gestures or fast animation; comfortable reading.
- **Trust concerns:** accuracy and dignity of cultural/historical content; correct names and spellings; respectful handling of sensitive history.
- **Likely next action:** find an event, view a gallery, contact the organization.

## 4. Teacher or student

- **Motivation:** learning or teaching about Crimean Tatar history and culture for a class, project, or personal study.
- **Likely entry point:** search, an educational-resources page, or history/language sections.
- **Key questions:** What are the reliable facts? Can I use this in a lesson or paper? Are there sources and materials?
- **Desired content:** clear history, sourced facts, a timeline, downloadable or citable resources, glossary/phrases, credible references.
- **Usability needs:** well-structured, citable, printable/downloadable; clear sourcing; accessible.
- **Trust concerns:** factual accuracy and citations; wants to distinguish established fact from interpretation.
- **Likely next action:** use/download a resource, cite the site, contact for more materials.

## 5. Researcher or journalist

- **Motivation:** background research, a story, or academic inquiry; needs accurate, attributable information and contacts.
- **Likely entry point:** search, About/Press, history, or contact page.
- **Key questions:** Who runs this? What's verifiable? Who can I interview? How does this differ from CACT?
- **Desired content:** organization facts, history with sources, media/press area, clear contact, high-resolution images with usage terms.
- **Usability needs:** quick access to facts and contacts; clear provenance; easy to reference.
- **Trust concerns:** credibility, sourcing, and the clear cultural-vs-advocacy (CACT) distinction; accurate attribution.
- **Likely next action:** contact the organization, cite/quote, request materials or an interview.

## 6. Cultural partner or institution

- **Motivation:** exploring collaboration (a screening, exhibit, event, or educational partnership).
- **Likely entry point:** About, Events/Films, or a partnership/contact page.
- **Key questions:** What does the organization do? Are they credible and active? How do we collaborate or host a screening?
- **Desired content:** clear program descriptions, past events, partner list, screening/partnership request path, professional contact.
- **Usability needs:** professional, trustworthy presentation; obvious contact/partnership route.
- **Trust concerns:** legitimacy, reliability, and reputational fit; clear separation from partisan advocacy.
- **Likely next action:** submit a partnership/screening request, contact the organization.

## 7. Potential volunteer, donor, or event attendee

- **Motivation:** wants to support the work or attend something — practical intent to act.
- **Likely entry point:** Events, Support/Get Involved, or a shared event link.
- **Key questions:** How can I help or give? What's coming up? Is my support used well? Is giving safe and (if applicable) tax-receiptable?
- **Desired content:** upcoming events with clear details, simple ways to volunteer/donate, transparency about the organization and its use of support.
- **Usability needs:** frictionless forms; clear, trustworthy calls to action; mobile-friendly; accessible.
- **Trust concerns:** legitimacy and safety of donation/registration; clarity on where support goes; not political fundraising.
- **Likely next action:** register for an event, donate, or sign up to volunteer.

---

## Cross-cutting design implications

- **Range of tech comfort and age** → mobile-first, large readable type, high contrast, restrained motion, simple navigation (serves profiles 1, 3, 7 especially).
- **Authenticity matters to insiders and outsiders alike** → culturally specific design, accurate content (profiles 2, 3, 4, 5).
- **Credibility and CACT distinction** → clear identity, sourcing, and contact (profiles 1, 4, 5, 6).
- **Action-readiness** → events, screening/partnership requests, volunteer/donate paths must be easy to find (profiles 6, 7).

## Open question for stakeholders

Which one or two of these groups is the **priority** for the first launch? The answer strongly shapes navigation, homepage emphasis, and which content must be ready first.
