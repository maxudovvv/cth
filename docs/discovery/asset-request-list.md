# Visual & Content Asset Request List — Crimean Tatar Heritage Canada

> For the organization. This is what we need from you to build the website, and in what form. **Please do not send us anyone else's photos, artwork, music, or copyrighted material until we've confirmed together that permission is in place.** Send what you have; we'll follow up on the rest.

## How to send

- Group files by type in clearly named folders (e.g. `logo/`, `events-2024-festival/`).
- Send **original, highest-quality** files — not screenshots or downloads from Facebook.
- Include a short note per item: what it is, when, where, who's in it, who took it, and whether we may publish it.
- Large files: a shared drive link (Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer) is fine.

## Image quality guidance (quick reference)

- **Photos:** JPG/PNG, at least **2000px** on the long edge for anything featured; more is better. Originals, not compressed re-shares.
- **Logo & tamga:** **vector (SVG/AI/EPS)** preferred; otherwise PNG with a transparent background at the largest size you have.
- **Posters/covers:** PDF or high-res image.
- **Audio/video:** highest quality you have; we can compress for the web.
- Avoid: screenshots, phone photos of printed photos, low-resolution social-media downloads (we'll ask for originals instead).

## Priority (what unblocks the most, soonest)

1. Confirmed **name** + **logo/tamga** + colors → lets design begin.
2. **"Who are the Crimean Tatars"** facts/sources + mission → lets the core pages be written.
3. One or two **cleared, high-quality images** → hero and key pages.
4. **Upcoming events** → a non-empty Events page.
5. **Contact recipient** → the site can actually connect people to you.

Everything else can follow in stages. Thank you.
