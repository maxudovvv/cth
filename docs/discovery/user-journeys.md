# User Journeys — Crimean Tatar Heritage Canada

> Status: **draft — preliminary journeys.** Maps how key visitors move through the site and, critically, **where each journey fails if content is missing.** Because most real content is not yet cleared (see `content-inventory.md`), most journeys are currently **content-blocked** — noted per journey.

Format: entry → path → success → **failure point if content unavailable**.

---

## 1. Discover who the Crimean Tatars are

- **Entry:** Home or search.
- **Path:** Home → "About the Crimean Tatars" overview → history/traditions taster → next step (events or learn more).
- **Success:** visitor leaves with a clear, respectful understanding and a sense of the culture.
- **Fails if:** no written, verified overview exists yet (**blocked** — needs sourced copy + cultural review). Without it the homepage is atmosphere with no substance.

## 2. Learn about Crimean Tatar history

- **Entry:** Home, Heritage hub, or search.
- **Path:** Heritage → History → (later) timeline → sources.
- **Success:** an accurate, sourced history at a comfortable depth.
- **Fails if:** history text is unsourced or unwritten (**blocked** — every claim needs a citation; sensitive periods need organizational approval).

## 3. Explore language and traditions

- **Entry:** Heritage hub.
- **Path:** Heritage → Language / Traditions → phrases, customs, music, food.
- **Success:** visitor engages with authentic cultural detail.
- **Fails if:** no verified phrases, recordings, or traditions content exists (**blocked**). Risk of filling gaps with generic clichés — must be avoided.

## 4. Find an upcoming cultural event

- **Entry:** Home, Events, or a shared social link.
- **Path:** Events → filter/scan upcoming → event detail → (register or add to calendar).
- **Success:** visitor knows what, when, where, and how to attend.
- **Fails if:** no upcoming events are provided with dates/locations (**blocked** — needs a real event list). An empty Events page is worse than none.

## 5. View photographs from past events

- **Entry:** Gallery, event detail, or social link.
- **Path:** Gallery → event album → captioned photos.
- **Success:** visitor sees community life, captioned and credited.
- **Fails if:** Facebook photos aren't cleared, captioned, and consented (**blocked** — SRC-FB-01/02 are pending permission; do not publish). Highest-volume potential content but currently unusable.

## 6. Find a film screening

- **Entry:** Films & Books, Events, or search.
- **Path:** Films → film detail → upcoming screening → attend/ticket link.
- **Success:** visitor finds a screening or the film's availability.
- **Fails if:** no film records or screening dates exist, or rights are unconfirmed (**blocked**).

## 7. Learn about translated books

- **Entry:** Films & Books.
- **Path:** Books → title detail → how to read/buy/borrow.
- **Success:** visitor discovers translated works and how to access them.
- **Fails if:** no book list, covers, or rights confirmation (**blocked**).

## 8. Request a film screening or partnership

- **Entry:** Films & Books, About, or Partners.
- **Path:** → "Request a screening / partner with us" → form → confirmation.
- **Success:** request reaches the organization and sender gets confirmation.
- **Fails if:** no form exists or no recipient/inbox is defined (**needs a form + a monitored destination email**). Also needs a described process.

## 9. Volunteer

- **Entry:** Support / Get Involved, Events.
- **Path:** → volunteer info → sign-up form → confirmation.
- **Success:** volunteer interest captured and routed to a person.
- **Fails if:** no defined volunteer roles or recipient (**blocked** — needs org input on roles and who receives requests).

## 10. Donate or support the organization

- **Entry:** Support, Home CTA, event pages.
- **Path:** → Support → donation method → external/processor → receipt.
- **Success:** visitor donates and (if applicable) receives a tax receipt.
- **Fails if:** no donation platform, charitable status, or receipt process confirmed (**blocked** — depends on legal/charity status). Do not fake a donation flow.

## 11. Contact the organization

- **Entry:** Contact, footer, About.
- **Path:** → contact details / form → message sent → confirmation.
- **Success:** visitor reaches the organization; message routed to a monitored inbox.
- **Fails if:** no public contact details or monitored destination (**needs confirmed email/recipient**). This is the minimum viable "action" and should be prioritized.

## 12. Understand the difference between the cultural organization and CACT

- **Entry:** About, or any page where the distinction is relevant.
- **Path:** About → clear statement of CTHC's cultural/educational identity → (optional) respectful note/link about CACT as a separate organization.
- **Success:** visitor understands these are distinct organizations with different missions.
- **Fails if:** the distinction isn't stated, or CACT is referenced without agreed wording (**needs stakeholder decision on treatment/wording** — see questionnaire D).

## Summary of blockers by journey

| Journey | Primary blocker | Owner to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 Learn/history/traditions | Verified, sourced cultural copy | Org + content-historian |
| 4 Events | Real upcoming event list | Org |
| 5 Galleries | Photo permissions + captions + consent | Org |
| 6–7 Films/books | Titles + rights confirmation | Org |
| 8 Screening/partnership request | Form + monitored recipient + process | Org + build |
| 9 Volunteer | Roles + recipient | Org |
| 10 Donate | Charity status + platform + receipts | Org |
| 11 Contact | Public details + monitored inbox | Org |
| 12 CACT distinction | Agreed treatment/wording | Org (stakeholder) |

**Takeaway:** The two journeys that can succeed soonest with least content risk are **Contact (11)** and a written **"Who are the Crimean Tatars" overview (1)** — provided the overview passes cultural review. Almost everything else waits on organization-supplied content and permissions. This directly informs the MVP recommendation.
